Prism texture pipeline

Downloads CC0 photo-scanned textures from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), shrinks and packs them, and saves them to assets/textures/ through the dev server. Albedo: NAME_a.webp (sRGB colour). Detail: NAME_n.webp (R, G: the normal's x and y, OpenGL convention; B: height). Each at 1024 and 512 (_a5, _n5). manifest.json lists them with their average colour (linear) so the engine can keep the world's own colours and take only the detail.
